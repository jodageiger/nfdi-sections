# Working Group Materials Inventory

Training offerings and materials already exist, but are often only known or accessible to a small circle of people (e.g. Train-the-Trainer concepts for RDM or Carpentries materials). This working group identifies such offerings within the consortia and other RDM projects, discusses them an produce good practices regarding the management of OER collections.

In addition to involving all NFDI consortia, communication with initiatives outside the NFDI is essential — for example, state-level RDM initiatives or data competency centres — to further disseminate and utilise their offerings.
